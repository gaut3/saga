Saga · saga_theme.dart · WCAG 2.1 AA

Text-hierarchy token retune

New fgMuted / fgSubtle / accent-text values for all four themes, with computed ratios against both grounds. Ink, Cream and Onyx keep the alpha-token approach; Terra gets darker grounds. Every proposed value clears 4.5:1 on both bg and surface, so the on-surface rule can be retired.

Strategy — four findings, four answers

01 · FGSUBTLE

Raise the floor, keep alpha

fgSubtle stays a real-content tier (it carries the 12px nav labels). Alphas move from 40–55% to 52–80% per theme — the minimum that clears 4.5 on the lighter of the two grounds, plus margin.

02 · TERRA

Darken the grounds

No text color passes on #C25A3A (cream 3.65, ink 4.14 — the mid-tone caps everything below 4.5). Recommendation: bg → #8E3A22, surface → #7A301B; #C25A3A stays as the theme's non-text hero color.

03 · CREAM

Two-step raise, not one

The 99→A6 nudge does clear fgMuted (5.22) — but fgSubtle then can't pass anywhere below it (min passing alpha is 9C). So both move: muted → B3, subtle → A0, order preserved.

04 · ACCENT

New accentText tier

One rule: accent is for large/bold text (≥3:1) and non-text UI; a new accentText passes 4.5 at any size. Ink/Onyx reuse amber as-is; Cream and Terra get one new hex each.

Ink

bg #1E1410 · surface #261B16 · fg #F4EAD8

One change. fgMuted already passes comfortably and keeps its 65%. fgSubtle moves 40% → 52% (0x84) — still clearly the quietest step against muted's 65%.

Terra → Ember — the decision

bg #C25A3A → #8E3A22 · surface #9E4128 → #7A301B

The mid-tone is unfixable as a text ground: full cream tops out at 3.65 and full ink at 4.14 — no color in either direction reaches 4.5 on #C25A3A. Retuning alphas, going opaque, or enforcing the on-surface rule all dead-end (even 87% cream on the current surface only just reaches 4.5). So the recommendation is structural but small: shift both grounds one step darker into rust, keep cream text, and let #C25A3A live on as the theme's identity color in non-text roles — hero blocks, seek bar fill, button fills, cover scrims. The darker cut also gets a new display name: Ember — warm coals rather than raw terracotta, and it sits naturally beside Ink, Cream and Onyx. Rename is display-only; the persisted index is untouched.

Recommended · Option A

Darken the grounds, rename to Ember

bg #8E3A22 (cream hits 6.34), surface #7A301B (7.77). The darker-surface structure survives; the on-surface rule is retired because everything now passes on bg. Hierarchy is deliberately compressed — fg 100%, muted 85%, subtle 80% — a bold theme reads bold, and all three tiers clear AA. Accent-as-text becomes pale amber #F0C48C (4.67 / 5.73); ink stops being used as text.

Fallback · Option B

Declare reduced-contrast

Keep #C25A3A exactly as shipped and label Terra "does not meet AA" in settings. Zero visual change, honest, and persisted-index-safe — but it leaves the 12px nav labels at 2.11 for anyone who picks it, and the audit finding stays open. Only worth it if the darkened rust reads as a different theme to you.

Pattern fields (§02) are opacity-governed texture and unchanged; the sticker's #9E4128 ground can move to #8E3A22 for consistency whenever it's next exported.

Accent-as-text policy

Split the role instead of darkening every accent. accent keeps its current values for non-text UI and large/bold text (18px+, or 14px+ bold — 3:1 floor). A new accentText is the only accent allowed at body sizes like the 13px "Full details" links, and must clear 4.5 on both grounds. In Ink and Onyx it aliases accent (amber already passes big); Cream and Terra each get one new hex, both drawn from the existing palette family.
